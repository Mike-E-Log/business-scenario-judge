# Third-party data and licenses

## MultiWOZ 2.2

Business-style customer-service scenarios in `data/gold_set.jsonl` are constructed
from the MultiWOZ 2.2 dialogue corpus (MIT License).

- Source: https://github.com/budzianowski/multiwoz
- Citation: Budzianowski et al., "MultiWOZ — A Large-Scale Multi-Domain
  Wizard-of-Oz Dataset for Task-Oriented Dialogue Modelling" (EMNLP 2018);
  2.2 update: Zang et al., "MultiWOZ 2.2: A Dialogue Dataset with Additional
  Annotation Corrections and State Tracking Baselines" (ACL 2020 NLP4ConvAI).
